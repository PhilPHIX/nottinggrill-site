    Good to know

    before you order.

    Where and when do you deliver?

    Wednesday 25 to Saturday 28 November, in a one-hour slot: Kensington 9am–12pm, Notting Hill from 12pm, St John's Wood in the afternoon until 6pm (7pm on Wednesday and Friday). If you're anywhere else, please reach out and we'll see how we can help: justin@nottinggrill.com.

    How much cooking is there?

    Very little. Everything arrives fully cooked, so it just needs reheating: the turkey comes roasted and rested with Justin's card for reheating it in your oven, and the sides warm through alongside it. Pies are ready to serve.

    Allergies?

    The veggie Wellington can be made gluten free for £5 a head (tick the box under the Wellington sizes). Pie ingredients and allergens are listed under each pie; sides marked V are vegetarian. Add any allergies at checkout and the team will be in touch.

    How do I pay?

    In full when you book, securely through Worldpay. Your confirmation arrives by email.

    Cooking for 16 or more?

    Email justin@nottinggrill.com and we'll size the turkey and the Wellington for your table.
