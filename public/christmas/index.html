      Good to know

      before you order.

      When is the last day to order?

      Friday 18 December. Kitchen slots are limited, so earlier is better.

      Where and when do you deliver?

      London: Monday 21 to Thursday 24 December, in a three-hour window you choose at checkout. Notting Hill £10, Kensington £15, Central London £20, Greater London £35. Across England: next-day courier for £45, arriving on 21, 22 or 23 December. Someone needs to be home to receive it.

      How much cooking is there?

      Very little. Everything arrives fully cooked and just needs reheating: the turkey comes roasted and rested with Justin's card for warming it through, and the sides go in alongside it.

      Allergies?

      The veggie Wellington can be made gluten free for £5 a head (tick the box under the Wellington sizes). Pie allergens are listed under each pie. Add any allergies at checkout and the team will be in touch.

      How do I pay?

      In full when you book, securely through Worldpay. Your confirmation arrives by email.

      Cooking for 16 or more?

      Email justin@nottinggrill.com and we'll size the turkey and the Wellington for your table.

      Ordering for a company?

      Use the corporate quote above. 10–19 feasts are 10% off, 20–39 are 15% off and 40 or more are 20% off. We deliver to one address or to each person's home.

      Can I order Thanksgiving too?

      Yes, until 21 November: order Thanksgiving here.
